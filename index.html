About Me

I would describe myself as a "be to yourself" person. I enjoy my own company, and at the same time, I value the company of people when it comes my way. Life has, in many ways, taught me to be comfortable with myself — to look within, reflect, and find peace and joy rather than constantly seeking them in the world around me.

I feel deeply grateful for what life has given me. I often look at my journey with a sense of gratitude and feel that God has been generous to me in many ways.

My journey has taken me through its share of ups and downs, both academically and professionally. From studying Mechanical Engineering at NITK Surathkal to pursuing Operations Research & Data Analytics at IIT Kharagpur, my path has taken turns I could not have predicted earlier.

I spent nine years at Bajaj Auto, an experience that taught me much more than engineering. Working under pressure and navigating demanding situations made me discover a resilience in myself that I did not know I had. Sometimes, when I look back at those years, I genuinely wonder, "Was that really me?"

Beyond academics and work, I am drawn towards things that allow me to slow down and look within. I enjoy playing tabla, although I am still very much a beginner. I have a growing interest in spirituality and ancient texts such as the Vedas and Upanishads, even though I am still at the beginning of understanding them.

I am still figuring out what I want to become. Some days, I dream of the mathematical brilliance of Ramanujan; on other days, I am inspired by the creativity and vision of Steve Jobs. Perhaps I don't need to have the answer yet. For now, I am simply seeking knowledge, finding rhythm, and exploring within.
